# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Django (Python), Tailwind CSS, plain HTML/JS.

## Users

Residents of Baliwag City (primary, all ages), LGU staff and officials (secondary).

## Product Purpose

CityWatch digitizes the reporting and resolution of infrastructure and public safety concerns, bridging the gap between residents and the local government through radical transparency.

## Positioning

A trusted, official municipal tool that emphasizes real-time tracking and public accountability, differentiating itself from black-box government complaint forms.

## Operating Context

Residents filing reports on mobile or desktop when encountering civic issues (potholes, streetlights, flooding). LGU staff reviewing and updating statuses.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must support real-time status tracking (Pending, Acknowledged, In Progress, Resolved, Closed), photo uploads, and location (Barangay) tagging. Must be highly accessible and avoid generic "startup" or "AI slop" aesthetics.

## Brand Commitments

Official Baliwag City identity: Baliwag Green (#156b3e) as primary, Blue (#004785) as secondary.
Typography: Outfit (headings) and DM Sans (body) for a friendly yet official civic tone.
Design Language: Clean, left-aligned structural authority; no excessive roundness or floating colored icon boxes.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Django models (Report, Announcement) and templates. Local database with 27 barangays.
