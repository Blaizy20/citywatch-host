About Baliwag City

Founded in 1733 and officially recognized as a city in December 2022, Baliwag has grown into one of the most progressive centers of commerce, transportation, and education in Northern Bulacan.

Known as the "Buntal Hat Capital of the Philippines," the city beautifully balances its rich Spanish-era heritage with modern civic development and transparent governance.
